## Reading the work without flattening it

A useful operating model should stay legible while it is being used. The structure should help the reader understand what happened, what mattered, and what can be carried forward.

## What the viewer should get

The goal is not just to describe systems. The goal is to give other engineers a usable way to think about changes, dependencies, and the tradeoffs that appear when the work is real.

## Why the record matters

The journal exists to preserve the reasoning behind the work. It keeps the human context visible so the lesson is not lost when the incident is over.

## Carrying the lesson forward

When the structure stays readable, the experience remains practical. The viewer can move from the narrative to the operational detail without losing the thread.
